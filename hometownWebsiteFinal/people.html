Our People


Between 2020-2024 the U.S. Census Bureau for Ann Arbor estimated about 122,036 people live in the community.

Within that survey they also asked the citizens questions about their ages.Using this they were able to determine that the average resident is between the eighteen to thirty-four.

with about 48.6% of their population fitting into that demographic.

The University

Of course, the focal point of our city is the university, which draws in a lot of young adults however that doesn't mean our other demographics are neglected. Our population is overwhelmingly progressive; our people promote a lot of activism or volunteer work.

Nature

Not to mention how environmentally friendly our people push to be, often people are seen riding bikes instead of taking vehicles. Our community is culturally diverse as well, mainly from the schools once again but it brings in all kinds of students and families from all over the world
