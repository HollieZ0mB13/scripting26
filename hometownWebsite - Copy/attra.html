What to do in Ann Arbor

There is a never-ending number of experiences to have in Ann Arbor, and we can guarantee that you'll make special memories in our city.


Outdoors

There are many bike trails, fishing spots, and places to hike if you're into the outdoors.

Places to Visit

If you're more into the arts there are many museums and art galleries to visit that bring in all sorts of artists, one of our galleries is the Michigan Art Gallery. If you like to shop until you drop, we also offer several exciting places to support local businesses like at Schmidt's antiques! There are also many community events that bring people together for markets or farmers markets.
