For my research there were a handful of websites that I looked at as a starting point to how I put everything into my own words. Thanks to their research, pictures and descriptions of the town I was able to bring this town to life in my own way.

  • Destination Ann Arbor - Ann Arbor, MI - This page describes the destinations and different streets the city has to offer.

  • Geographic Names Information System Ann Arbor - National Map - This page is agovernment page and is a geological survey.

  • About Us - Ann Arbor Art Center - This page is about the Ann Arbor Art center, it shows galleries, artists opportunities and more.

  • City of Ann Arbor - A2Gov - This page is the official Ann Arbor page; it mainly is used for citizens to pay off bills or taxes however it also has information about the town and things to do as well

  • Ann Arbor, MI Demographics - Every City in the USA - This page has the demographics of every city in the USA, from the U.S. Census Bureau's from 2020-2024.

  • Living in Ann Arbor - University of Michigan - This page is the official website for the University of Michigan; this specific spot helps students learn about things they can do in the city and what it's like living in Ann Arbor.

  • Top Reginal Employers - Ann Arbor USA - This page details the top employers for Ann Arbor

  • All Businesses - Kerrytown District of Ann Arbor, Michigan - This website lists all the businesses in the Kerrytown District of Ann Arbor
