Ann Arbor's

Geography

The Landscape

In Ann Arbor the landscape and the nature enclosing the city are very important to our residents. The gorgeous trees, fast bodies of water and stunning valley's captivate the hearts of many of our citizens. And we can ensure you will experience this the moment you visit us.


The Forestry

Even in the city you will be surrounded by a thick forest of trees, within the city there it's more than 50,000 trees. Due to our high density of forestry, it's become a trademark for us and the community.

The Valley's

Even if you aren't into nature, it will certainly grow on you after you start living here, finding comfort among the many hills and valleys we have to offer

Bodies of Water

There's a little something for everyone here so even if trees aren't your thing, perhaps the lakes are? Ann Arbor happens to be surrounded by a vast amount of water since we're located right along the Hudson River. Which runs through the southeast part of the city as it trickles its way down to Lake Erie.
