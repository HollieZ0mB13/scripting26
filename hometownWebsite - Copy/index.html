If you were looking for a beautiful, charming city located in Michigan then Ann Arbor is the perfect place for you. There are so many things to do in this oasis of a city, since we offer a variety of activities. We have parks, swimming pools, shopping malls, concert venues, and more! Making it the perfect destination for people of all ages. A big part of what makes our tight nit community stand out is getting to be the home of the University of Michigan. Which brings a lively energy to our town and downtown districts. A big part of our community is getting to show support for our students; the community's favourite is getting to attend the school's football games since it's an NCAA school.
